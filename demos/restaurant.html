Our story

Three generations, one table

Luigi Moretti arrived from Bologna with a suitcase and his mother's recipe book. Thirty-eight years on, his grandchildren roll the same tagliatelle at the same marble counter.

Every dish starts the way it did in 1987: pasta dough rested overnight, ragù cooked low and slow for six hours, and bread baked twice a day in our stone oven.

We keep the menu short on purpose — fourteen dishes we would serve our own family — and we change the specials with the seasons and the market.

Chef: Marco Moretti · Seats: 64 · Private dining: The Cellar Room, up to 18 guests

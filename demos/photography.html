Behind the camera

Hi, I'm Elena Marchetti

Twelve years, 300+ weddings, one simple rule: no stiff poses, no interrupting the day — just gentle direction and an eye for the in-between moments.

I started Golden Hour Studio after photographing my sister's wedding in a Brooklyn backyard and realising the photos everyone framed weren't the posed ones — they were the tears, the laughter, the squeeze of a hand before the vows.

That's still how I work. I shoot quietly, I scout the light before your guests arrive, and I deliver a gallery that feels like the day actually felt — warm, unhurried and true.

Based: Greenpoint, Brooklyn · Travel: NYC, Hudson Valley, the Catskills & beyond · Kit: Dual Canon bodies, film on request

— Elena

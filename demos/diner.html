Our story

Fifty years on the same corner

Three generations of the Papas family. Same batter recipe, same beef-dripping know-how, same Friday-night queue.

George Papas opened The Golden Catch in 1974 with a single range, a sack of Maris Pipers and a batter recipe he refused to write down. His son learned it by eye; his granddaughter runs the shop today — and the recipe is still only in their heads.

Our fish comes in fresh from Billingsgate every morning, our potatoes are cut (never frozen) in the shop, and everything is fried to order. It takes a few minutes longer. Regulars will tell you it's worth it.
